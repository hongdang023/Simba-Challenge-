# Product Requirements Document

## My Personal Website Challenge Website

**Brand:** Nemo12 · Sư Tử Con School  
**Program:** AI Teen  
**Document type:** Competition information website PRD  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Primary audience:** AI Teen students  
**Secondary audience:** Parents and prospective Nemo12 families  
**Competition deadline:** 23:59, Sunday, 06/09/2026

---

## 1. Product Summary

The My Personal Website Challenge website is a lightweight, one-page information hub for the AI Teen competition.

The website has three jobs:

1. Explain the challenge clearly.
2. Direct students to the correct submission locations.
3. Explain the rewards, rules and timeline in a way that parents can also understand.

The website is **not** a submission system and **not** a showcase gallery. Students submit their Website/Product and 4F Reflection through Simba. Students upload their video to Google Drive or YouTube and send the link in the AI Teen Zalo group.

---

## 2. Objectives

### 2.1. Student objectives

After reading the website, a student should understand:

- What the challenge question means.
- What website they need to create.
- What evidence they need to prepare.
- Where each part must be submitted.
- How to choose and verify a domain.
- What the three featured awards recognise.
- Where to read the detailed Website and 4F rubrics.

### 2.2. Nemo12 objectives

The website should:

- Make the competition feel clear, exciting and achievable.
- Reduce repeated explanations in the Zalo group.
- Drive students to the Website/Product section on Simba.
- Drive students to the separate 4F Reflection section on Simba.
- Communicate Sư Tử Con’s learning philosophy: students create, test, reflect and improve.
- Present the domain prize and three featured awards clearly.

### 2.3. Success metrics

- Students can identify the three required submission components.
- Students can locate the correct Simba submission destinations.
- Students understand the deadline and completion requirements.
- The competition receives submissions from the AI Teen cohort.
- Students who complete all requirements receive a domain for one year.
- The website reduces clarification questions about rules and submission.

## 4. Information Architecture

The site should be built as a one-page website with anchor navigation.

```text
My Personal Website Challenge
│
├── Overview
├── Challenge
├── How to Join
├── Rules
├── Awards
└── FAQ
```

### Primary navigation

- Overview
- Challenge
- How to Join
- Rules
- Awards
- FAQ

### Primary CTAs

1. **Make Your Website** → Simba / Products section.
2. **Do Your Reflection** → Simba / 4F Reflection section.

The video is submitted separately through Drive/YouTube and Zalo. It should be explained clearly on the site but does not need a separate primary CTA.

## 5. Page and Section Requirements

### 5.1. Overview

#### Purpose

Create immediate understanding of the challenge and motivate students to start.

#### Required content

- Brand mark: Sư Tử Con.
- Label: **AI Teen Challenge**.
- Title: **My Personal Website Challenge**.
- Challenge question:

  > Nếu có một góc trên Internet để giới thiệu thế giới của mình, em muốn cho người đó xem gì?

- Short description:

  > Tạo một website cá nhân bằng Google Antigravity, giới thiệu thế giới của mình với một người xem cụ thể.

- Deadline: **23:59, 06/09/2026**.
- Prize highlight: **Mỗi học sinh hoàn thành đủ yêu cầu nhận một domain cá nhân trong 1 năm.**
- Primary CTA: **Make Your Website**.
- Secondary CTA: **Do Your Reflection**.

#### Design direction

The hero should feel warm, encouraging and student-centred. It should not feel like a high-pressure coding contest.

---

### 5.2. Challenge

#### Purpose

Explain what students are expected to create.

#### Required content

Heading:

> Em sẽ tạo sản phẩm gì?

Explain that the website should help one specific viewer understand:

- Who the student is.
- What the student likes or cares about.
- A product, activity, achievement or experience the student is proud of.
- How the viewer can learn more about the student.

Include two simple examples:

1. A website for a new classmate who likes football, introducing the student’s favourite game, school life and a product they created.
2. “My World” for a parent, including favourite games, favourite subjects, a day at school and a small game created by the student.

Add a clear note:

> Website không cần giống bài mẫu. Điều quan trọng là con hiểu người xem, giải thích được lựa chọn của mình và có bằng chứng phù hợp.

CTA: **Read the Website Guide on Simba** / **Make Your Website**.

---

### 5.3. How to Join

#### Purpose

Show the full workflow and make the three submission channels unmistakable.

#### Five-step process

1. **Choose a Viewer**  
   Chọn một người xem cụ thể.
2. **Plan Your Story**  
   Nghĩ xem người đó muốn biết gì thêm về con.
3. **Build with Antigravity**  
   Tạo website với Google Antigravity.
4. **Choose Your Domain**  
   Kiểm tra domain trên Cloudflare, lưu bằng chứng và giải thích lý do chọn.
5. **Test & Improve**  
   Cho người xem trải nghiệm, ghi lại phản hồi và nâng cấp website.

#### Three submission components

| Component | Where to submit | What to submit |
|---|---|---|
| Website/Product | Simba | Website link, product thinking answers and evidence |
| 4F Reflection | Simba | Facts, Feelings, Findings and Future |
| Introduction Video | Drive/YouTube → Zalo | Video link sent in the AI Teen group |

Use a visual distinction between the two Simba CTAs and the separate Zalo video instruction.

#### CTA placement

- After the Website/Product explanation: **Make Your Website**.
- After the 4F explanation: **Do Your Reflection**.
- Video instructions should be presented as a simple numbered instruction, not as a third main platform flow.

---

### 5.4. Rules

#### Required rules

- Participants are AI Teen students.
- Each student submits an individual project.
- Students use Google Antigravity.
- Website must be deployed on Cloudflare Pages.
- Deadline is 23:59 on 06/09/2026.
- Students need one primary domain and one backup domain.
- Students must check domain availability on Cloudflare and submit evidence.
- Students must explain why they chose the domain.
- Website must include at least three content sections and three real images.
- Students must ask a viewer for feedback and record it.
- Students must explain at least one improvement and why they made it.
- Students must create a 60–90 second video with their real face, real voice and website screen.
- Students must complete the 4F Reflection on Simba.

#### Assessment note

Add the following note:

> Assessment rubric chi tiết của Website nằm trên trang nộp bài Website/Product của Simba. Rubric chi tiết của 4F Reflection nằm trên trang 4F Reflection của Simba. Con hãy đọc rubric để hiểu thế nào là một bài làm tốt.

Do not duplicate the full rubrics on the competition site.

---

### 5.5. Awards

#### Everyone Can Earn

Every student who completes all required components receives:

- One personal domain for one year.
- Recognition for completing My Personal Website Challenge.

#### Top 3 Featured Awards

Three different students receive one title each:

##### Best Website Experience

For the website that creates the clearest value and most suitable experience for its audience.

##### Best Website Story

For the strongest combination of personal story, domain rationale and introduction video.

##### Best Reflection

For the 4F Reflection that most clearly shows what the student learned, did and will do next.

#### Extra benefits for Top 3

Each Top 3 student also receives:

- An additional **200,000 VND** prize.
- Opportunity for the work to be selected as a model submission on Simba.
- Featured placement on the Sư Tử Con website.
- Inclusion in suitable Nemo12 communication materials.

#### Award rule

Each student can receive a maximum of one featured title. The three titles should be given to three different students.

---

### 5.6. FAQ

Group questions into four categories.

#### The Challenge

- Can I choose a different topic from the examples?
- Does my website have to look like the sample?
- What counts as a personal website?

#### Domain

- Where do I check whether a domain is available?
- What should I do if my first domain is no longer available?
- Why do I need a backup domain?

#### Video

- How long should the video be?
- Do I need to show my face?
- Does the video need advanced editing?
- Where do I submit the video?

#### Submission and Reflection

- Where do I submit the website?
- Where do I submit 4F Reflection?
- Can I save my work and finish later?
- Is a high AI score the most important part of 4F Reflection?

Suggested answer for the last question:

> Không. Điều quan trọng nhất là con hiểu mình đã học được gì, làm được gì và sẽ làm gì tiếp theo. AI score chỉ là gợi ý để con và mentor nhìn lại bài reflection.

## 6. Content and Tone

### Tone

- Warm.
- Clear.
- Encouraging.
- Student-centred.
- Concrete rather than promotional.
- Friendly to middle-school students.

### Copy principles

- Use short sentences.
- Explain technical terms in plain Vietnamese.
- Use “con” when speaking directly to students.
- Use “học sinh” or “các bé” in parent-facing explanations.
- Explain the reason behind each requirement.
- Avoid language that implies students must beat one another.

### Core message

> Đây không chỉ là một cuộc thi làm website. Đây là cơ hội để con tạo ra một góc Internet thật sự thể hiện mình, rồi nhìn lại xem mình đã học được gì trong quá trình đó.

## 7. Visual Design System

### 7.1. Brand colours

| Colour | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Sunset Orange | `#DB6718` | Primary brand colour, key accents, primary CTA |
| Lion Orange | `#F58220` | Secondary CTA, highlights, interactive states |
| Savannah Gold | `#E5AD18` | Supporting accents, badges and award highlights |
| Cub Cream | `#F2C077` | Soft highlight surfaces and decorative accents |
| Midnight Blue | `#172A33` | Main headings, strong text and contrast areas |
| Sand Beige | `#FBF8F1` | Warm page background |

### 7.2. Colour rules

- Use Sand Beige as the primary page background.
- Use white or very light cream cards for content sections.
- Use Midnight Blue for headings and important text.
- Use Sunset Orange for the main CTA and primary brand moments.
- Use Lion Orange for secondary actions and interactive emphasis.
- Use Savannah Gold for awards, deadline highlights and small accents.
- Maintain strong contrast between text and background.

### 7.3. Logo rules

- Keep clear space around the logo at least equal to the lion’s head height.
- Use the original SVG or approved AI logo asset.
- Use the orange background version on photographs or visually busy backgrounds.
- Do not change the logo colour, add shadows, add gradients, distort its proportions or rewrite “SƯ TỬ CON”.
- Minimum logo width: 120px on screen and 25mm in print contexts.

### 7.4. Layout

- Use generous rounded cards.
- Use thin warm borders rather than heavy shadows.
- Use a clear vertical rhythm between sections.
- Use icons sparingly to make steps and requirements easier to scan.
- Keep the page readable on desktop, tablet and mobile.

### 7.5. Suggested visual components

- Hero challenge card.
- Countdown/deadline badge.
- Five-step process cards.
- Three submission cards.
- Award cards.
- Completion checklist.
- FAQ accordion.
- Sticky or repeated Simba CTA area.

Do not use a student gallery or website preview grid on this site.

## 8. Functional Requirements

### Navigation

- Anchor navigation scrolls to the correct section.
- Active section state is visible where practical.
- Navigation remains usable on mobile.

### CTA behaviour

- The Website CTA opens the configured Simba Products URL.
- The Reflection CTA opens the configured Simba 4F Reflection URL.
- External links open reliably and should be clearly identified.
- CTA URLs must be configurable without rewriting page content.

### FAQ

- Questions expand and collapse.
- Only the answer content changes; page layout should remain stable.
- FAQ remains readable on mobile.

### Responsive design

- Desktop, tablet and mobile layouts are required.
- CTA buttons should remain easy to tap on mobile.
- No horizontal scrolling.
- Text must not be clipped or overlap.

### Accessibility

- Use semantic headings in order.
- Provide accessible labels for buttons and icons.
- Ensure keyboard navigation for navigation and FAQ.
- Maintain sufficient colour contrast.
- Do not communicate requirements through colour alone.

## 11. Definition of Done

The website is ready to publish when:

- All six navigation sections are present.
- The challenge question and deadline are visible above the fold or immediately discoverable.
- The two Simba CTAs point to the correct destinations.
- The three submission components are explained correctly.
- Video instructions clearly state face, real voice, website screen and 60–90 seconds.
- Domain rules include primary domain, backup domain, Cloudflare evidence and one-year period.
- The three awards and their additional benefits are clearly explained.
- The full Website and 4F rubrics are not duplicated; students are directed to Simba for details.
- No showcase or leaderboard is included.
- All FAQ answers match the Competition Rules document.
- The site works on desktop, tablet and mobile.
- All external links work.
- Keyboard navigation and colour contrast are acceptable.
- Nemo12 approves the final copy and links.
